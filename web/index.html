          Honest limitations

          • Trained on a 21 % subset of ASVspoof 2019 LA; no cross-dataset test yet.
          • Voice-conversion attacks A17/A18 remain hard, and misses can be confident.
          • Robust to noise only because of augmentation; real codecs (MP3/Opus) are untested.
          • Grad-CAM is coarse (≈2 kHz × 0.5 s) and explains the CNN branch only.
          • A screening aid — not proof. Confirm high-stakes findings with a human examiner.

          Data & credits

          • ASVspoof 2019 LA database (Todisco et al., 2019), ODC-BY. Sample clips come from its evaluation set.
          • Grad-CAM: Selvaraju et al., ICCV 2017. EfficientNet-B0: Tan & Le, 2019.
          • Uploaded audio is analysed in memory and not stored; only metadata (verdict, score, length, latency) is saved.
